For members

Your cellar, in your pocket.

If your venue keeps your bottles, Horreum is how you see them: every bottle with its story, the map of where they came from, and one tap to have one brought to your table.

Sign inHelp getting started

The Collection

Every bottle in your locker, drawn as a cellar sheet: the tasting note, the drink window and whether it is at its peak, and how to serve it — temperature and decanting, worked out for that bottle. Print it or share it.

The Atlas

An interactive globe of the world’s wine regions. Your own bottles are pinned to where they were made; tap a region to see what grows there and how far it is from your table.

History

What you have enjoyed and when, what came into your locker and how, and any requests you have made — with the receipt on any bottle you bought in the app.

Ratings

Rate a bottle the moment you have enjoyed it — one tap on the stars, right in your history — and add a few words if you like. Your reviews live with the bottles they belong to.

Show your server

Tap a bottle in your collection and a code appears. Your server scans it and is walked straight to your bottle — no searching, no guessing.

Requests and messages

Ask for a bottle from your venue’s list, or send the team a note. Announcements from the venue arrive in the app.

Getting started

Three steps, one minute.

  • Your venue invites you. An email arrives with a link to set up your account.
  • Confirm it is you. Enter your email and the six-digit code we send it; then choose a password.
  • Install it. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap Install app. It opens like any other app, and it remembers you.

Two themes — classic by day, midnight after dark — or let it follow the sun where you are.
